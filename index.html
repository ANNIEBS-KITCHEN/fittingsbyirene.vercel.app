Frequently Asked Questions

1. How far in advance should I book my fitting?

We recommend booking 3-4 months before your event for bridal gowns, and 2-3 weeks for evening wear. Sunday bookings are available via WhatsApp.

2. Do you provide fabrics or do I buy them?

We can do both! We source premium fabrics for you, or you can bring your own. During your consultation we’ll guide you on the best fabric for your design.

3. How many fittings are included?

All custom gowns include 2-3 fittings to ensure a perfect fit. Additional fittings are available on request.

4. Do you offer rush orders?

Yes! Need it faster? Ask about our 1-week rush service for evening wear. Rush fees apply and depend on availability. Message us on WhatsApp to check.

5. Do you deliver outside Benin City?

Yes! We ship nationwide. We also do virtual consultations for clients outside Edo State. Delivery fees apply.

6. Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the balance due at final fitting. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss options.
