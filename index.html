About Us

Fittings by Irene creates luxury custom dresses for queens. We specialize in royal traditional marriage attire and red-carpet gowns, but we also make cocktail dresses, evening dresses, dinner dresses, church outfits, birthday dresses, photoshoot dresses for any special occasion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the gowns custom made?

A: Yes. Every gown is tailored to your exact measurements.

Q: How long before my event should I order?

A: We recommend 1-2 weeks for custom pieces.

Q: Do you do rentals or sales only?

Q: Do you deliver outside Edo State?
